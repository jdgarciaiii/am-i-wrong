Privacy Policy

D&C Studio  ·  Effective date: June 1, 2026  ·  Last updated: June 1, 2026

We don't store your data, we don't sell it, and we don't track you.

1. Who We Are

Am I Wrong? is developed and operated by D&C Studio. If you have any questions about this policy, contact us at privacy@dcstudio.app.

2. What Information We Collect

We collect minimal information necessary to provide the service:

  • Dispute text — the two sides of your conflict that you enter into the app. This is processed to generate your verdict and is not stored.
  • Basic usage data — limited technical information such as request frequency, used only to ensure fair access and prevent abuse. This is not linked to your identity.

We do not collect your name, email, location, or device identifiers.

3. How We Use Your Information

The text you submit is used solely to generate an AI-powered verdict for your dispute. It is not stored, shared for marketing purposes, or used for any purpose other than generating your result.

4. Third-Party AI Processing

Am I Wrong? uses an AI service to generate verdicts. Your dispute text is processed by this service and is not used to train AI models. No personal information is shared with third parties for advertising or marketing purposes.

5. Data Retention

We do not retain your dispute text after your verdict is generated. Your content exists only for the duration of processing and is discarded immediately after.

6. Children's Privacy

Am I Wrong? is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13. If you believe a child has used our app, please contact us.

7. Security

We take reasonable technical measures to protect your information. All data is transmitted securely and we do not store user-submitted content.

8. Your Rights

Since we do not store your personal data, there is nothing to delete or export. If you have privacy concerns, please reach out to us directly.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be reflected with an updated date at the top of this page. Continued use of the app constitutes acceptance of any updates.
